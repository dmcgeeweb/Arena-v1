Redevelopment

A €38m rebuild of
Ireland’s home of indoor sport

South Dublin County Council granted planning permission for a full redevelopment of the Arena. The plans take the building to 8,000 square metres, double the seated capacity, and add four courts, a gym, a café and corporate facilities.

What changes

Twice the seats, four times the courts

The redevelopment keeps what the building is good at — one large, flat, accessible floor — and adds the capacity and back-of-house that a national venue needs.

  • CapacitySeated capacity roughly doubles, to 3,272
  • CourtsSpace for four basketball courts
  • TrainingA gym within the building
  • Front of houseCafé and a new entrance concourse
  • BusinessCorporate facilities and offices for Basketball Ireland
  • AccessFully accessible and inclusive throughout
The Arena will be “transformative for Irish indoor sport”.
John Feehan, Chief Executive, Basketball Ireland

Not just basketball

The plans have the backing of several other national governing bodies who want to use the building — Volleyball Ireland, Badminton Ireland and Irish Squash among them.

That matters for commercial hire too. A venue built to serve several indoor sports is a venue built for flexible floor use, which is the same quality an exhibition or conference organiser is buying.

Where it stands

Progress so far

Planning is secured. Funding is the remaining piece, with an application made to the Large Scale Sport Infrastructure Fund.

  1. Design and planning application

    Scheme developed for the Tymon Park site and lodged with South Dublin County Council.

  2. Planning permission granted

    South Dublin County Council notified Basketball Ireland of its decision to grant permission for the scheme.

    September 2024

  3. Funding

    Financing is the key remaining element, including an application to the Government’s Large Scale Sport Infrastructure Fund. A start date follows from that.

    In progress

  4. Construction

    Works are expected to take around 18 months once they begin.

    18 months

Booking the Arena during the works

The Arena is taking bookings as normal and a construction start date has not been set. If your event falls in a period that could be affected we will tell you before you commit, and we will hold an alternative date where we can. Check a date or call +353 1 459 0211.

Why it matters

Every booking already funds the game

The Arena is a not-for-profit venue. Income from hire filters back through Irish basketball, and a bigger, better building generates more of it — at a point when the registered playing population grew by more than a fifth in a single year.
